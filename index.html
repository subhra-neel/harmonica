How to read harmonica tabs


12345678910


Holes are numbered from left (low) to right (high).




4 or ↑4Blow (breathe out) into hole 4


-4 or ↓4Draw (breathe in) through hole 4


-4'Bend: draw hole 4 and lower the pitch. Each ' is one more half-step down


4<Chromatic only: blow hole 4 with the slide button pushed in


[1.2.3]Chord: blow holes 1, 2 and 3 together. [-2.3.4] = draw them together


· and /A rest, and the gap between beats. | marks the bars




Practising


    

  • Play lets you hear the part. Loop a few bars and slow it down with Speed.
    • 

  • Perform listens while you play along, after a one-bar count-in. You get a score, stars and tips.
    • 

  • Only your own playing counts: Play, Replay and speaker playback never add to your progress, and a lesson counts only when you perform all of it.
    • 

  • Aim for 80% (★★) before moving on. Speed trainer raises the speed as you improve.
    • 

  • The app checks pitch and timing. It cannot judge tone or breathing, so listen to yourself too.
    • 



Good to know


    

  • Use a quiet room and keep the tablet about an arm's length away.
    • 

  • If your timing always shows early or late, run Calibrate in Settings.
    • 

  • Set your harmonica's key in Settings. The tabs stay the same; the notes the app listens for change.
    •
