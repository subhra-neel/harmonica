MIDI to harmonica tabs

Load a .mid file (song, keyboard or drums), pick a track and your harmonica key.

Analyses the first 2.5 minutes. Works best on a clean solo recording.
Piano roll: bars show the notes the harmonica will sound, with note name and tab. Red outline = not playable.
Tap a note in the roll to fix it.
Pick a section: tap the bar numbers at the top of the roll twice (start, then end), or use the menus. Press Play to loop it. Tap a tab below to hear it and see it on the harmonica.
Orange = blow, blue = draw, dashed = button pushed in, faded = bends. A tab in brackets is a chord: [1.2.3] = blow holes 1, 2 and 3 together, [-2.3.4] = draw holes 2, 3 and 4 together. Names are the real pitches of your harmonica key.
